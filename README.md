# Ivadoo

Ivadoo is a modular open-core ERP platform built for fashion, textile and garment businesses.

It is designed for fashion houses, tailoring workshops, ready-to-wear brands, uniform manufacturers, textile companies, fabric distributors and multi-site fashion groups.

The main Ivadoo modules include:

- CRM and customer management
- Measurements and made-to-measure workflows
- Products, materials and variants
- Collections, designs and PLM
- Sales and orders
- Inventory and warehouses
- Purchasing and suppliers
- Manufacturing and MRP
- Quality control
- Employees and teams
- Subcontracting
- Delivery and returns
- Expenses, finance and accounting
- Reporting and business intelligence

Ivadoo modules are designed to work together as one integrated system, from customer acquisition and product design to production, delivery and profitability analysis.

The platform is built around a modular, multi-company and multi-establishment architecture, with support planned for managed cloud, private server and on-premise deployments.

## Project status

Ivadoo is currently in active development.

Phases 0 through 2 are complete and merged. Phase 3 — Operations is active. The platform extensibility foundation covering module activation, custom fields, runtime metadata and generic CSV/XLSX data I/O is also merged into `main`.

Implementation continues through the Phase 3 roadmap on `phase/3-operations` after validated cross-cutting platform gates are synchronized with `main`.

## Documentation

Project documentation and module specifications are maintained in the `docs/` directory. The functional and technical specification remains the primary requirements reference until more detailed module specifications are validated.

## Security

Security is a core requirement of Ivadoo. The platform is designed around strict organization isolation, role-based access control, auditability and protection of sensitive business data.

Security reporting procedures will be documented before the first public release.
