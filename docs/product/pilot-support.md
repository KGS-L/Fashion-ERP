# Pilot support baseline

Status: `accepted` during Phase 0.

## Official channels

The first FashionERP pilot uses the following official support channels:

- a dedicated support email address;
- a private ticketing system.

WhatsApp may be used only as a complementary coordination or urgency channel. It is not the official support history and must not be used to transmit sensitive business data.

## Diagnostic data

The Control Plane must not copy customer business data by default. Support diagnostics should therefore use only the minimum technical information necessary, such as:

- FashionERP version;
- deployment/environment identifier;
- error or correlation identifier;
- relevant technical logs after minimization of sensitive data;
- installation and module information required to reproduce the issue.

Any exceptional access to customer data must be explicit, authorized, limited in scope and handled according to the project security requirements.

## Support record

Important support decisions, reproduction steps and resolution status must be recorded in the private ticket so that the support history remains auditable and is not fragmented across informal messaging channels.
