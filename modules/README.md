# Business modules

Ivadoo is organized around modular business domains.

Planned modules include:

- Core platform
- CRM and customers
- Measurements and made-to-measure
- Products, variants and materials
- Collections, designs and PLM
- Sales and orders
- Inventory and warehouses
- Purchasing and supply
- Manufacturing and MRP
- Quality
- Employees and teams
- Subcontracting
- Delivery and returns
- Expenses and management finance
- Accounting
- Reporting and BI

Detailed specifications for each module will be added progressively before implementation.
